Here's what your 2025 sales data shows. All figures are **net revenue**: what you actually received after discounts and refunds.

## What I cleaned up

- **Removed 18 duplicate orders** created when the three platforms were combined. This left **620 real orders**. These duplicates are likely why your numbers felt off.
- **Fixed the same thing being written in different ways.** For example, "London", "LONDON" and "london" are now one region, and product names are consistent.

- **Applied your 10% and 15% discount codes**, so revenue reflects what customers really paid.
- **Nothing real was deleted.** Returns are kept and clearly marked, and orders with no customer ID are labelled "Guest".

## Key findings

**1. You earned £21,133 in 2025.** Discounts reduced sales by £891, and 25 returns cost you £880 in refunds, about 4% of sales, which is a healthy level.

**2. Almost half your year happens in October–December.** These three months brought in about 48% of your revenue. November was your best month (£3,697), and January the quietest (£812). *This is when stock and marketing matter most.*

**3. Your premium products earn the most.** The Cotton Throw Blanket, Woven Storage Basket and Macrame Wall Hanging bring in 44% of revenue. Most products sell in similar numbers, so the higher price is what makes these three stand out. *The Woven Storage Basket is your star: it's both your most popular item (113 sold) and one of your top earners.*

**4. London and the South East are your core market.** Together they make up 52% of revenue. North West customers appear to spend a little more per order (about £39 vs £32–34 elsewhere), but there are fewer orders there, so this is worth watching rather than acting on yet.

**5. Your website is your strongest channel.** It brings in 56% of revenue, Etsy 32% and Instagram 12%. Instagram orders are smaller, at about £27 on average vs about £35 on the website and Etsy, likely because shoppers buy one cheaper item on impulse.

## Recommendations

1. **Prepare for October early.** Order extra stock of the blanket, basket and wall hanging by September, and plan your main marketing for October–November.
2. **Feature premium items on Instagram.** Show the basket and blanket in posts, or try a bundle (e.g. candle + basket) to lift the average Instagram order.
3. **Consider growing your Storage and Decor ranges.** Each is a single product that earns more than some whole categories. A second basket size or another wall hanging is a low-risk way to grow.
